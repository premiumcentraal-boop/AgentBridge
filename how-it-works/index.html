HONEST ABOUT COMPATIBILITY

What needs to work?

Carrier forwarding is established technology. Compatibility with a particular Grok Bot account and calling plan still needs a live verification.

Grok Bot account

Native outgoing US calls must be available on your account, and the phone service must permit this use case. AgentBridge cannot enable calling inside another product.

Telecom carrier

The account must be approved for the requested destination, have balance or credit, and expose an assigned US access number.
