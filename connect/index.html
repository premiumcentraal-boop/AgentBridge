A simple connector is all an agent should need. The instructions below describe our planned integration; public access has not been activated.

STEP 01

Understand the connection

When available, you'll receive a secured AgentBridge MCP URL and a scoped access token. Your account will need an assigned US access number and approved calling balance.

This is an independent Grok Bot integration, not an official Grok/xAI feature or a bypass of their calling availability restrictions.
STEP 02

Give Grok Bot a simple instruction

Use this prompt after your AgentBridge account has been provisioned. Do not paste secrets in ordinary chat.

Connect my AgentBridge remote MCP server using the authenticated integration settings I provide securely. When I ask you to call an international number, first use prepare_international_call. Confirm the destination and cost before making a native US outbound call to the returned bridge number. You must speak on the call yourself. Never substitute another voice agent.
BRING YOUR OWN PROVIDER

Connect your own SignalWire project.

Developers can run a read-only check of a SignalWire Project ID and the voice-enabled US numbers already assigned to their project. Credentials remain server-side. The signed calling endpoint currently hangs up safely; paid international forwarding is not activated.

Read the SignalWire developer setup. Native Grok Bot calling still needs a consent-based real-world test.
