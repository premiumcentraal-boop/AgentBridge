A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

The details
matter.

Is AgentBridge another AI voice agent?+

No. AgentBridge is a telephone routing layer. Grok Bot remains the only conversational speaking AI. The product does not record or transcribe your calls.

Can I place a call today?+

No. You can explore the product, manage route drafts, and connect a separately configured private backend for read-only setup checks. Native Grok Bot dialing, carrier authorization and end-to-end live calling remain unverified.

What can I do in the studio?+

Save and archive destination drafts, view your activity, choose Simple, Guided or Advanced guidance, and inspect a configured backend. The interactive demo is temporary; authenticated studio records are stored on the server.

Is this an official Grok or xAI product?+

AgentBridge is an independent Victor project. It is not affiliated with Grok, xAI, Cursor or Apple. Any eventual use requires the calling platform’s permission and compliance with its terms.
